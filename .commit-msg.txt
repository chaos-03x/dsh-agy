fix(translate): structurally deny the request shape upstream rejects

The community bisected the trigger to a PAIR, not a phrase: `requestType:
"agent"` TOGETHER WITH a non-empty `request.systemInstruction` is answered with
a fabricated 429 on every endpoint, while either field alone is 200 - and it is
independent of the text and its length. Our body sent both, so every turn that
carried a system prompt was a guaranteed 429 reporting itself as "quota
exhausted".

- translate: the system text ALWAYS rides the first user turn and the
  `systemInstruction` field is never emitted. The escape-hatch option is gone: a
  switch here would only switch between "works" and "fails 100% of the time".
- translate: `neutralizeSystemText` rewrites the literals the filter is also
  known to key on (client identity claims, the dual-use security sentence,
  upper-case MUST/REQUIRED/NEVER/ALWAYS). Meaning preserved, magic words
  changed.
- adapter: the env switch and its method are removed with it.

Verified live: a system prompt containing "You are Claude Code", MUST/REQUIRED
and the dual-use sentence now returns 200 on gemini-4-hydrogen-high, where it
429'd before. 704 tests pass; four of them pin this shape (systemInstruction
absent, phrases neutralised, agent flag still present).
